  • device:view
  • alarm:view

  • protocol:simulate

  • POST /api/devices
  • GET /api/devices
  • GET /api/devices/{id}/telemetry
  • GET /api/devices/{id}/status
  • GET /api/device-alarms
  • POST /api/device-alarms/{id}/ack
  • POST /api/protocol-adapters/modbus/simulate
  • POST /api/protocol-adapters/opcua/simulate

状态标签

  • Online
  • Offline
  • Running
  • Stopped
  • AlarmTriggered
  • AlarmAcked
  • AlarmRecovered

  • 模拟 MQTT 上报
  • 模拟 Modbus
  • 模拟 OPC UA
  • 确认告警
  • 查看关联工单

告警详情冻结字段

  • 告警编号、设备编号、告警类型、等级、触发时间、确认人、恢复时间
